The Journey

1
Tue 8 Sep · Day 1
Hà Nội — Arrival
Arrive Noi Bai on IZ595 (14:05). Guide meets at exit column 14. Transfer to Somerset Grand Hanoi.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi
2
Wed 9 Sep · Day 2
Hà Nội — City Highlights
HCM Mausoleum complex, One Pillar Pagoda, Tran Quoc Pagoda on West Lake, Temple of Literature, Old Quarter, hidden café & famous egg coffee.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi
3
Thu 10 Sep · Day 3
Hà Nội — Cooking Class at a Garden House
Cyclo ride to market, Thanh Ha wet market tour, serene garden house with fish pond & vegetable garden, hands-on cooking class. Menu: nem, bún bò, bò cuốn lá lốt, phở gà, palm sugar cake.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi
4
Fri 11 Sep · Day 4
Hà Nội → Mai Châu
3-hour scenic drive southwest. Afternoon village walk, chat with White Thai hill tribe, hands-on turmeric fabric dyeing.
🏨 Mai Chau Ecolodge
5
Sat 12 Sep · Day 5
Mai Châu — Mai Hịch Village & Valley Cycling
Bicycle through the valley past stilt houses and bamboo groves, stop to watch farmers plant or harvest rice. Afternoon at leisure.
🏨 Mai Chau Ecolodge
6
Sun 13 Sep · Day 6
Mai Châu → Pú Luông
Morning cycling through rice fields, then 1.5hr drive to Pú Luông. Afternoon: hanging bridge walk, bamboo rafting on the dam, iconic giant water wheels.
🏨 Pu Luong Retreat
7
Mon 14 Sep · Day 7
Pú Luông — Valley, Weavers & Hieu Waterfall
Descend the S-shaped scenic road to the valley. Uoi Village panorama, Lan Village weaving craft family visit, bicycle through farmland, ponds & mountains to Hieu Waterfall in the Nature Reserve.
🏨 Pu Luong Retreat
8
Tue 15 Sep · Day 8
Pú Luông → Ninh Bình
3-hour drive. Visit Bích Động cave temple. Afternoon sampan through Tam Cốc's three river caves, village trail to Thai Vi temple.
🏨 Emeralda Resort Tam Coc
9
Wed 16 Sep · Day 9
Ninh Bình — Farmer Encounter & Hang Mua
Village visit, roll up your sleeves with a local farmer (fish, crabs, rice bailing). Home-style lunch. Afternoon: 465 steps up Hang Mua Dance Cave for panoramic "Ha Long Bay on Land" views.
🏨 Emeralda Resort Tam Coc
10
Thu 17 Sep · Day 10
Ninh Bình → Hạ Long Bay — Board Cruise
3.5hr drive to Ha Long. Board Athena Royal Cruise 12:45. Sail to Bai Tu Long Bay, kayak Cong Do area, cooking demo on deck, happy hour, dinner under the stars.
🛳️ Athena Royal Cruise
11
Fri 18 Sep · Day 11
Hạ Long Bay — Cruise Day 2
Tai Chi at dawn. Day boat to Vũng Viêng floating fishing village — pearl culturing, kayaking through the village, traditional lunch aboard, tea ceremony.
🛳️ Athena Royal Cruise
12
Sat 19 Sep · Day 12
Hạ Long → Hà Nội → Fly to Huế
Thiên Cảnh Sơn cave (200 steps!), brunch buffet, disembark 10:45. Drive to Hanoi (2.5hrs). Fly HAN→HUE 16:35→17:55. Guide meets at exit gate.
🏨 Senna Hue
13
Sun 20 Sep · Day 13
Huế — Imperial City
Cyclo ride to UNESCO Citadel, Phap Lam royal cake-making session, Khai Dinh Tomb (127 steps, spectacular dragon pillars & mosaic interiors), Thiên Mụ Pagoda.
🏨 Senna Hue
14
Mon 21 Sep · Day 14
Huế → Hải Vân Pass → Sơn Trà → Hội An
Breathtaking coastal drive over Hải Vân Pass with Lang Co Beach views. Sơn Trà Peninsula — Goddess of Mercy at Linh Ứng Pagoda, douc langur monkeys. Arrive Hội An mid-afternoon.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
15
Tue 22 Sep · Day 15
Hội An — Ancient Town & Countryside
Cam Thanh coconut village basket boat, Old Town walking tour & Japanese Covered Bridge, silk lantern making. Evening: Hội An night market, flower lanterns released on the Hoai River.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
16
Wed 23 Sep · Day 16
Hội An — Bà Nà Hills & The Golden Bridge
50-min drive to Bà Nà Hills (1,400m). Cable car through the clouds, the iconic Golden Bridge cradled in giant stone hands, Fantasy Park & French Village.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
17
Thu 24 Sep · Day 17
Hội An → Fly to Hồ Chí Minh City
Morning at leisure. Driver to Đà Nẵng airport, fly to HCMC. Guide meets at exit gate A1–A2. Transfer to Somerset.
🏨 Somerset Ho Chi Minh City
18
Fri 25 Sep · Day 18
HCMC — City Highlights · 🔀 Families Split
Doritte & Richard — private transfer to airport, depart from HCMC to Tel Aviv.
Adi, Verly, Sol & Neil — Dong Khoi St, CIA building, Notre Dame, Central Post Office, Reunification Palace, FITO Museum of Traditional Medicine, Cholon & Thien Hau Temple.
🏨 Somerset Ho Chi Minh City
19
Sat 26 Sep · Day 19
HCMC → Mekong Delta → Vũng Liêm
3-hour drive to the Mekong. Cycle through duck farm, dragon fruit orchard, coconut factory, rice paddies. Learn pop rice making. Help cook a traditional home-cooked dinner at Coco Riverside Lodge.
🏡 Coco Riverside Lodge
20
Sun 27 Sep · Day 20
Vũng Liêm — Mekong Delta Life
Private boat canal cruise, floating market, Cao Dai temple, coconut workshop, sampan to Mother Hero's village, her grandson's frog farm, coconut juice with the family.
🏡 Coco Riverside Lodge
21
Mon 28 Sep · Day 21
Vũng Liêm → Hồ Chí Minh City
Harvest water coconut leaves, weave with the host, poon stick demo. Return to HCMC by private car. Final evening in Saigon.
🏨 Somerset Ho Chi Minh City
🏠

HCMC → Hà Nội → Tel Aviv
Fly SGN→HAN (12:00→14:10). Own way to international terminal. Board IZ596 at 19:50 → Tel Aviv 01:55 Wed 30 Sep 2026. Safe travels! 🪷

Huế — Imperial City & Royal Tombs
Vietnam's ancient imperial capital in full
▼
~08:00
🥐
Breakfast at Hotel
Excellent breakfast buffet at Indochine Palace — highly rated by guests.
~09:00
🏯
The Imperial Citadel & Forbidden Purple City
The UNESCO World Heritage Complex of Huế Monuments. Walled citadel modelled on Beijing's Forbidden City, built 1804–1833. Allow 2–3 hours to explore the palaces, temples, and cannon-lined ramparts. Note: Ladies should cover shoulders and knees inside the Citadel.
📍 Imperial Citadel
~11:30
⛩️
Thiên Mụ Pagoda
Seven-tiered Phước Duyên tower overlooking the Perfume River — Huế's most iconic landmark. The famous Austin car that carried monk Thích Quảng Đức to his 1963 self-immolation is displayed here.
📍 Thiên Mụ Pagoda
~13:00
🍽️
Lunch
Bún Bò Huế or a riverside restaurant near the pagoda.
~14:30
🪦
Royal Tombs along the Perfume River
Visit 1–2 of the royal tombs of the Nguyễn emperors. Khải Định Tomb is the most ornate (mosaics of ceramics and glass); Minh Mạng Tomb has serene garden landscapes. Each is ~30min by car south of Huế.
📍 Royal Tombs area
~19:00
🍽️
Dinner — Huế cuisine
Huế is Vietnam's culinary capital. Try Bánh Bèo (steamed rice cakes with shrimp), Bún Hến (clam vermicelli), or a royal cuisine set menu at the hotel. Early night — very early start tomorrow.
