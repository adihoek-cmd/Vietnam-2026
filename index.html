Day by Day

The Journey

1
Mon 23 Mar · Day 1
Tel Aviv → Hà Nội → Hội An
Arrive Hanoi on EY432 at 5:55am. Proceed to the domestic terminal and fly VN165 (10:10 → 11:35) to Đà Nẵng, then private transfer to Hội An.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
2
Tue 24 Mar · Day 2
Hội An — Cooking Class & Ancient Town
Bicycle through Trà Quế organic farm, join a hands-on cooking class, then explore the lantern-lit UNESCO old town at leisure.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
3
Wed 25 Mar · Day 3
Hội An — Bà Nà Hill & Golden Bridge
Full day excursion up to the French Hill Station at 1,487m and the famous Golden Bridge cradled in giant stone hands, with panoramic views to the sea.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
4
Thu 26 Mar · Day 4
Hội An → Đà Nẵng → Sơn Trà → Huế
Morning on the Sơn Trà Peninsula with the Giant Lady Buddha and rare red-shanked douc langurs, then drive north over the dramatic Hải Vân Pass to the imperial city of Huế.
🏨 Indochine Palace Hotel Hue
5
Fri 27 Mar · Day 5
Huế — Imperial City
Explore the magnificent UNESCO Citadel and Imperial Enclosure, the royal tombs along the Perfume River, and the iconic Thiên Mụ Pagoda.
🏨 Indochine Palace Hotel Hue
6
Sat 28 Mar · Day 6
Huế → Hà Nội — VN1540
Early morning — depart Phu Bai at 07:50, arrive Hanoi 09:10. Afternoon exploring Hoan Kiem Lake, the Old Quarter's 36 guild streets, and the Temple of Literature.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi Hotel
7
Sun 29 Mar · Day 7
Hà Nội → Hạ Long Bay 🛩️
Drive to Tuan Chau Marina. At 10:50 — 25-min scenic seaplane flight over Hạ Long Bay for 5 passengers (Doritte will skip the seaplane). Board Signature Cruise at noon, sail into the emerald waters of Bai Tu Long Bay. Kayaking through Vũng Viêng floating fishing village.
🛳️ Signature Cruise · Bai Tu Long Bay
8
Mon 30 Mar · Day 8
Hạ Long → Ninh Bình
Morning cave visit at Thiên Cảnh Sơn — 200 steps up through spectacular stalactites. Brunch buffet on deck while cruising to port, then disembark and drive south to the karst landscape of Ninh Bình.
🏨 Emeralda Resort Tam Coc
9
Tue 31 Mar · Day 9
Ninh Bình
Full day exploring Vietnam's ancient former capital — sampan boat through the Tam Cốc river caves, cycling through emerald rice paddies, visiting Bích Động Pagoda and the Hoa Lư citadel ruins.
🏨 Emeralda Resort Tam Coc
10
Wed 1 Apr · Day 10
Ninh Bình → Mai Châu
Drive west into the highlands to the peaceful Mai Châu valley, home of the White Thai ethnic minority — stilt houses, handwoven silk, and rice fields ringed by limestone mountains.
🏨 Mai Chau Ecolodge
11
Thu 2 Apr · Day 11
Mai Châu — Village Hike & Rafting
Pickup at 9:30 for a hike to remote Mai Hịch Village through rice meadows and limestone mountains. Meet Thai farmers, learn traditional crafts, then end the day bamboo rafting along a tranquil stream.
🏨 Mai Chau Ecolodge
12
Fri 3 Apr · Day 12
Mai Châu → Hà Nội
Leisure morning, then 3.5-hour drive back to Hanoi. Final evening in the capital — night market, last Vietnamese dinner, maybe a bia hơi on the pavement.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi Hotel
13
Sat 4 Apr · Day 13
Hà Nội — Free Day
A final free day at your own pace. Wander the Old Quarter, visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum complex, grab last-minute gifts at Đồng Xuân Market, or simply soak it all in over a café trứng.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi Hotel
🏠
