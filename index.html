Dinner — Hanoi Downtown Restaurant ✅
Table booked for 6, 18:00–19:30. Booked under Doritte Hoek. 09 Ô Quan Chưởng St, Hoàn Kiếm — about a 15-minute walk from the Somerset, at the edge of the Old Quarter by the ancient gate. Contact: Ms. Mai Phuong Anh, +84 914 430 209 (WhatsApp).
Must order
  • Chả cácha caTurmeric-and-dill fish finished in a pan at your table. Hanoi's own dish — barely exists anywhere else in Vietnam.
  • Bún chảbun chaCharcoal-grilled pork patties dropped into a sweet-sour broth, with vermicelli and a mountain of herbs.
  • Nem cua bểnem cua beSquare-wrapped crab spring rolls, the Hanoi version. Reliable with children.
  • Cá nướngca nuongGrilled fish — one of the dishes reviewers single out here.
The kitchen serves each private room together so nobody waits, and they take pre-orders — worth sending your choices ahead on a first night after fifteen hours of flying. Full vegetarian menu for Doritte. Five private rooms across three floors of a hundred-year-old merchant house.

Must order

Dinner is reserved for you both nights. The restaurant is the first floor of the main stilt house, open-sided over the valley; in the evening it's fireplace and candles, with folk music some nights.
