Day by Day

The Journey

1
Mon 23 Mar · Day 1
Tel Aviv → Hà Nội → Hội An
Arrive Hanoi on EY432 at 5:55am. Head to the domestic terminal for VN165 (10:10→11:35) to Đà Nẵng, then private transfer to Hội An.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
2
Tue 24 Mar · Day 2
Hội An — Cooking Class & Ancient Town
Bicycle through Trà Quế organic farm, join a hands-on cooking class with local farmers, then wander the lantern-lit UNESCO old town at leisure.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
3
Wed 25 Mar · Day 3
Hội An — Bà Nà Hill & Golden Bridge
Full day up to the French Hill Station at 1,487m and the famous Golden Bridge cradled in giant stone hands, with panoramic coastal views.
🏨 Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa
4
Thu 26 Mar · Day 4
Hội An → Sơn Trà → Huế
Morning on the Sơn Trà Peninsula with the Giant Lady Buddha and red-shanked douc langurs. Drive north over the dramatic Hải Vân Pass to the imperial city of Huế.
🏨 Indochine Palace Hotel Hue
5
Fri 27 Mar · Day 5
Huế — Imperial City
Explore the magnificent UNESCO Citadel and Imperial Enclosure, royal tombs along the Perfume River, and the iconic Thiên Mụ Pagoda.
🏨 Indochine Palace Hotel Hue
6
Sat 28 Mar · Day 6
Huế → Hà Nội — VN1540
Early morning flight — depart Phu Bai 07:50, arrive Hanoi 09:10. Afternoon exploring Hoan Kiem Lake, the Old Quarter, and the Temple of Literature.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi Hotel
7
Sun 29 Mar · Day 7
Hà Nội → Hạ Long Bay 🛩️
Scenic seaplane flight over Hạ Long Bay at 10:50 for 5 passengers (Doritte will skip). Board Signature Cruise at noon, sail Bai Tu Long Bay. Kayaking through Vũng Viêng floating fishing village.
🛳️ Signature Cruise · Bai Tu Long Bay
8
Mon 30 Mar · Day 8
Hạ Long → Ninh Bình
Morning cave visit at Thiên Cảnh Sơn (200 steps of stalactites!). Brunch buffet on deck, disembark, then drive south to the karst landscape of Ninh Bình.
🏨 Emeralda Resort Tam Coc
9
Tue 31 Mar · Day 9
Ninh Bình
Sampan boat through Tam Cốc river caves, cycling through emerald rice paddies, Bích Động Pagoda, and the ancient Hoa Lư citadel ruins.
🏨 Emeralda Resort Tam Coc
10
Wed 1 Apr · Day 10
Ninh Bình → Mai Châu
Drive west into the highlands to Mai Châu valley — home of the White Thai minority, stilt houses, handwoven silk, and rice fields ringed by limestone peaks.
🏨 Mai Chau Ecolodge
11
Thu 2 Apr · Day 11
Mai Châu — Village Hike & Rafting
Pickup 9:30 for a hike to remote Mai Hịch Village through rice meadows. Meet Thai farmers, learn traditional crafts, then bamboo rafting along a tranquil mountain stream.
🏨 Mai Chau Ecolodge
12
Fri 3 Apr · Day 12
Mai Châu → Hà Nội
Leisure morning, then 3.5-hour drive back to Hanoi. Final evening — night market, last bia hơi on the pavement.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi Hotel
13
Sat 4 Apr · Day 13
Hà Nội — Free Day
A final free day — wander the Old Quarter, Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Đồng Xuân Market, or just linger over a café trứng.
🏨 Somerset Grand Hanoi Hotel
🏠
Sun 5 Apr · Day 14 — Homeward
Two Departures from Hà Nội
Doritte & Richard — pickup 08:30, depart BR398 at 12:05
Adi, Verly, Sol & Neil — pickup 15:45, depart EY431 at 19:40 via Abu Dhabi → Tel Aviv
